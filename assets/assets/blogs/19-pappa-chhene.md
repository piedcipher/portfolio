# Pappa Chhe Ne

Today, Mom is taking her first flight. She recently turned 70, and seeing her begin this new adventure fills me with pride. Beneath that joy is an ache: Dad should be here to share this moment with her. He passed away in 2019, and I still wish I could tell him about the life Mom and I have built, and see his smile as she sets off.

પપ્પા, મેં સારી જગ્યાએ નોકરી કરી, ઘરનું ધ્યાન રાખ્યું, પણ તમે નહોતા.

મેં નવું ઘર લીધું, પણ તમે નહોતા.

મેં મમ્મીની સંભાળ રાખી, પણ તમે નહોતા.

પણ મનના ઊંડાણમાં મને ખબર હતી, પપ્પા છે ને.

Mom's first flight is hers, but I dedicate this milestone to both of you. I wish Dad could be beside her today. I hope the sky carries her gently, and that she feels how much love is traveling with her. I still carry him with me, in every new beginning and every moment I wish I could share with him.
