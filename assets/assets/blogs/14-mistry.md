# Mistry

First proper Hyderabad + Goa trip.

And honestly — Goa and Hyderabad were the best trip of my life. Also my first time visiting both.

This was for Samvid’s bachelor party. Team was Nikhil, Samvid, Rahul, and me. Full boys trip.

## Hyderabad

First stop was Hyderabad.

Very different vibe, but really good start to the trip.

Did a **Microsoft office visit**, then food scenes:

- **Nilofer bun maska**
- **Kaanchi Cafe dosa**
- **California Burrito** (amazing)

Also noticed Hyderabad societies are on another level with amenities. Saw stuff I hadn’t really seen before in normal residential setups.

Did a lot of walking there too, especially around the society area. Slower pace, more chill.

## Goa

Then Goa.

Started with **Zostel Morjim**. Great place, good crowd, always some movement.

Best part was late-night beach scenes. Just walking with no plan, talking nonsense, checking random spots, sitting for a bit, then moving again.

We went around **Chapora lane** and **Chapora beach** too. Saw that abandoned ship, spotted crabs around the side, crossed cracked patches/roads, and just kept roaming.

Also did the usual bar-side scenes, music, and late nights.

Then shifted to an **Airbnb in Anjuna**. Solid stay. Good location, chill setup.

Spent time at **Anjuna beach** too. Great vibe there.

Food was nice throughout:

- Burger Factory
- Bawri
- Jamun

Also did some shopping — picked up fridge magnets and kajus.

No complaints. Ate well, moved a lot, slept less.

## Bros

Special mention: **Nikhil and Rahul were Samvid’s roommates**, and both were amazing throughout the trip.

Easy company, no drama, always sorted.  
Trip was 10x better because of them.

## End

Overall, unreal trip.

Hyderabad was calm and sorted. Goa was chaos and energy.

Best trip of my life, with the right people, at the right time.

Perfect bachelor party for Samvid.
