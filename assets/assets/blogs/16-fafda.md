# Fafda

Today was Vijayadashami / Dussehra, so obviously jalebi-fafda scene was mandatory.

Had proper plate today — **fafda, jalebi, mirchi, kadhi**.  
Simple joy.

Ahmedabad on Dussehra morning is always a vibe.  
Crowds everywhere, fresh fafda getting packed nonstop, hot jalebi smell in the air, people waiting in lines but still happy.

Some famous spots that always come to mind:

- **Oshwal**
- **Saurashtra Farsan, Kalupur**
- **Swati Snacks**
- **Bhavnagari, Paldi**
- **Chandravilas**

All OG places.

Honestly this combo is more than food now. It’s memory.  
You eat one bite and immediately feel festival mode.

No big philosophy.  
Just good food, good day.
