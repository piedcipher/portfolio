# Fitbit

I ordered a Fitbit Air. Part of me is excited about having a new way to track my walks; another part knows the real challenge is simply getting back out there.

I was walking regularly through September and October, until I stopped on October 22. I had found a rhythm, and the walks had become a familiar part of my days. Camp Hanuman was one of my favorite destinations. I also made my way to the Riverfront, Madhupura Temple, and SBR.

Some walks turned into long adventures — 20 kilometers, even 30. The distance was never the whole point. It was the feeling of keeping going, seeing more of the city, and having time to think.

Now I’m ready to begin again. The Fitbit can keep track, but the first step is still mine to take.

Here’s to getting back to walking — and becoming the next Batman, Jack Reacher, or at least someone who remembers to charge their Fitbit.
