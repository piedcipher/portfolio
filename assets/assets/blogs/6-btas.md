# I'm tired, Alfred

This one gets me.

- [Alfred](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0956544/): Master Bruce, are you all right, sir?
- [Batman](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0175834/): I'm tired, Alfred.
- [Alfred](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0956544/): Well I shouldn't wonder, you've taken no meals today, and I can't recall when you last slept.
- [Batman](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0175834/): A weary body can be dealt with, but a weary spirit, that's something else. Sometimes, old friend, I wonder if I'm really doing any good out there.
- [Alfred](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0956544/): How can you doubt it? The lives you've saved, the criminals you've brought to justice.
- [Batman](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0175834/): I've put out a few fires yes, won a few battles, but the war goes on Alfred, on and on...

It’s the quiet part that gets me. The wear.
