# Pappa Chhe Ne

Today, Mom is taking her first flight. She recently turned 70, and seeing her begin this new adventure fills me with pride and joy. It also makes me wish Dad were here to see it. He passed away in 2019, but I still find him with me in every milestone.

પપ્પા, મેં સારી જગ્યાએ નોકરી કરી, ઘરનું ધ્યાન રાખ્યું, પણ તમે નહોતા.

મેં નવું ઘર લીધું, પણ તમે નહોતા.

મેં મમ્મીની કાળજી રાખી, પણ તમે નહોતા.

પણ મનના ઊંડાણમાં મને ખબર હતી—પપ્પા છે ને.

This first flight is Mom's, but in my heart, I'm celebrating Dad too. I wish he could be beside her today. Wherever she goes, I hope she feels how much love is traveling with her—and I hope he knows that I still carry him with me.
