# Blackout

Samvid's wedding was something I had been looking forward to for months. Spent days preparing with him and visited his home almost every day.

18 November, went to Indore. Good day.

19th was carnival and sangeet. Another good day.

20th, I drank too much. Smoke all around, a few painkillers too. I was also on Ozempic, which slows digestion. Blacked out and vomited all over the room.

I had gone back to my room, but everyone was looking for me. Mom was worried. They eventually found me.

Woke up feeling fine the next day. But I'm not proud of that day. I didn't spoil the function, but I did worry people who care about me.

Later, went around Indore. Chhapan Dukan, Khajrana Ganesh Mandir, Haldiram's and some old monuments.

Rahul and Nikhil took care of everything. Grateful to both of them.

Made a mistake.
