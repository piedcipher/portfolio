# Protein Protein Protein 🦇

In 2024, I decided to get serious about protein and calorie awareness. It's been a journey.

My goal wasn't to lift heavy or build a lot of muscle. I mainly wanted to lose weight while keeping my protein intake up.

I'm not a nutrition professional — this is simply what worked for me.

My weight started increasing because of stress and changes in my personal life. The biggest increases happened around 2016, 2018, and COVID.

I started at 105kg in 2024. By April 2026, I'd reached 75kg. The first drop from 105 to 95kg came relatively easy. 95 to 85 was challenging. 85 to 75 felt like the hardest stretch. Most of that was cardio — elliptical, cycling, walking, machine exercises. I was a regular gym-goer until July 2024, then pivoted mostly to diet. Home dumbbell work was sporadic at best.

I'm at 91kg now in 2026. The job loss and some personal changes threw things off, but I'm back to being intentional about what I eat.

These are some of the things I kept coming back to.

## Protein Powder

Whey protein is pretty simple. Most scoops have around 25g of protein, depending on the brand. I usually use it in a shake when I don't have much time or when I'm short on protein for the day.

I've used:

- [MuscleBlaze Biozyme Performance Whey](https://www.muscleblaze.com/sv/muscleblaze-biozyme-performance-whey/SP-88093) — Chocolate and Kesar Thandai are solid. 

- The [WholeTruth protein range](https://thewholetruthfoods.com/categories/protein-powder-pro) is good too. Coffee and Mango flavours are my favourites. Their muesli is worth trying, and the cranberry protein bars are surprisingly good.

- I also liked [SuperYou Vanilla](https://superyou.in/products/vanilla-fermented-yeast-500g), along with their protein wafer bars.

## Paneer Chillas

In 2024, I used to make chillas with 200g of paneer, besan, curd, onion, capsicum, green chilli, and tomato. I added cumin, jeera, pepper, red chilli powder, turmeric, and coriander. They were filling and easy enough to make regularly. In 2026, I switched to low-fat paneer.

## Tempeh and Whey Rotis

In 2024, I also had 200g of tempeh cooked with vegetables, followed by three whey rotis made with [WholeTruth unflavoured whey](https://thewholetruthfoods.com/categories/protein-powder-pro). Together, it was around 70g of protein and 800 calories. The tempeh was about 40g of protein and 450 calories, while the rotis were around 30g and 350 calories.

[MillD High Protein Atta](https://instamart.in/p/milld-high-protein-atta-L2DCEP12U6) is a separate high-protein atta option. Amul has one too.

The weight's creeping back up to 91 now. The intention is still there though. It's not a sprint.
