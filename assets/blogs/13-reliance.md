# Reliance

This one is about Dheeraj Lalwani.

Met him through Twitter. Mumbai guy, software engineer, solid energy. Clean, composed, and the kind of person who carries himself well without making a scene.

He got me into working out of cafés like Sip of Hope and OCD. I showed him some of my spots like Ciao Pizza, Madhavrao, and Nini's Kitchen. Good trade. His side, my side. Easy.

He’s been to my house a couple of times. We’ve met whenever he came by. He’s also introduced me to a bunch of mutuals, which is always a net positive.

We met again after some time.

The title is a wordplay on Dhirubhai Ambani and Reliance, and also on Dheeraj and Dhiru. Seemed right.

If friendship had a background score, this one would sit somewhere between Manhar Udhas, Batman keeping watch from a corner, Reacher walking through trouble, Silicon Valley overthinking everything, and TVF Pitchers trying to hold it together.

A mix of walking, gym, whey, work, and enough momentum to keep it moving.

That feels about right.
